title: "24 hours in one of the most beautiful cities in Europe"
series: "Eurotrip"
place: "Vienna"
excerpt: "Vienna was a quick stop, but we made the most of it."
---

We spent the whole day walking around the city, seeing some of the main historic sites, squares, churches and that unreal architecture that shows up on practically every corner.

And the day came with scorching sun. After wandering around the city for a long time, we ended up in a park by the river, where we could finally slow down, get in the water and enjoy the rest of the day.

It was rushed, but it was sick.
