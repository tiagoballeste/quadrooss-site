title: "Berlin was one of the best cities of the trip"
series: "Eurotrip"
place: "Berlin"
excerpt: "Berlin was one of the cities that surprised me the most on this trip. I arrived expecting something completely different, and in two days we got to enjoy the city a lot."
---

We went through Alexanderplatz, the Berliner Dom, the Brandenburg Gate, the Reichstag, the Bundestag, the Soviet War Memorial in Tiergarten, the Siegessäule, the Oberbaumbrücke, Treptower Park and, of course, the Berlin Wall. In between, we still had time to check out some stores, buy a few things and get a taste of Berlin's techno scene.

On the second day, we slowed down the sightseeing a bit to film some content for Run & Bass. Then we went shopping (sneakers, clothes and other stuff the guys wanted) and wrapped up our time in Germany heading straight to Amsterdam.

It was only two days, but Berlin turned out to be way more interesting than I expected.
