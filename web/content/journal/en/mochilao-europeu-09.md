title: "One last night with nowhere to sleep"
series: "Eurotrip"
place: "Madrid"
excerpt: "After crossing Europe, we went back to Madrid to live the last two days of the trip right where it all began."
---

And of course it wouldn't end in a normal way lol. On the first night, we had no hostel and decided to just make the most of the night. We went out, stayed up until morning and, when our bodies finally gave out, we ended up sleeping in the hostel lobby until we could check in.

On the last day, the mission changed completely: we went after the thrift stores and shops we still wanted to see, trying to find everything we'd left to buy before going back to Brazil.

And that's how the trip ended. After 16 days, several cities, miles of walking, bikes, trains, planes, sleepless nights, struggles and stories we'll probably be telling for a long time, we came back to exactly where we started.

Here's to many more like this. And I hope you, who watched it, get to live the same with the people you love.
