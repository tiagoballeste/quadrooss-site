---
title: "The last big night out of the trip"
series: "Eurotrip"
place: "Paris"
excerpt: "We got to Paris completely exhausted and found out our hostel was far from pretty much everything. The solution was to do all of Paris by bike, and that ended up being one of the best parts of the city."
---

After almost 30 minutes pedaling to the hostel, we dropped our stuff and went right back out. We went up to the Sacré-Cœur Basilica in Montmartre and from there started walking around the city without much of a plan. At some point the group split up: some went to Notre-Dame, others followed the Seine, until everyone met up again at the Eiffel Tower, where we ended the day having dinner together.

We still tried to find something to do at night, but the exhaustion of the last few days won.

The next day, it was bikes again. We rode all over Paris, part of the crew got separated from us and, along the way, even stumbled onto a techno party at some kind of protest we still don't know what it was about. Our time in the city ended up shorter than it could have been because we chose to fly to Madrid, so we had to leave Paris much earlier.
