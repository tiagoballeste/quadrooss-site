Come along with me and the guys from Run & Bass on the best trip of our lives so far.

I decided to turn this experience into a vlog series, recording everything I lived in each country. I only had one camera battery, so I filmed as much as I could every day, trying to balance living the trip and documenting every moment.

There will be 6 or 7 episodes following the 16 days we spent across Europe, discovering new places, living unexpected stories and carrying nothing but a backpack.

I hope you enjoy it as much as we enjoyed living it.
