series: "Eurotrip"
place: "Venice"
excerpt: "In the fourth episode of our Eurotrip, we arrived in one of the most unique cities in the world: Venice."
---

The plan was simple: spend one day getting to know the city. But the day felt like it would never end. We walked more than 20 km, crossed canals, saw historic spots like the Rialto Bridge and ended up in an unexpected mess involving public transport, a fine and a situation we still can't tell was a scam or just a lack of information.

After exploring every corner of Venice, we headed straight to Vienna, closing one more chapter of this backpacking trip across Europe, and one more night without a hostel, sleeping on the bus.
