We met up with Will, who flew out from São Paulo to join us in the middle of the trip. From there it was nonstop: the Vatican, the Colosseum, lunch, the Trevi Fountain and miles of biking across one of the most beautiful cities I've ever been to.

Rome was where I felt the most that sense of being exactly where I was supposed to be. An energy that's hard to explain.

At the end of the day, after almost a day and a half without a shower, we finally got one and headed to our next stop: Venice.

One more episode of our series showing the 16 days we spent traveling across Europe with nothing but a backpack.
