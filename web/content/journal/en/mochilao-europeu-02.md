We rode up to Montjuïc Castle, went down to the port, passed by the beach, ended up at the Sagrada Família and kept pedaling around Barcelona with no plan. Along the way, Brito still managed to break his phone lol.

There was a lot we didn't even have time to film. The idea was to live the city as much as possible, without forgetting that at 9:30 pm we were already boarding for Rome. So it was that balance between enjoying every minute and not missing the flight.

This episode is exactly that: a whole day riding around on bikes.
