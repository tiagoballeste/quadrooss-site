title: "We got lost in Amsterdam"
series: "Eurotrip"
place: "Amsterdam"
excerpt: "After two very intense days in Berlin, we got to Amsterdam already a bit tired, and I think that changed a lot about how we experienced the city."
---

We couldn't explore as much as we wanted, and Amsterdam might be the only city on this trip where we'd change the plan a little if we could go back. Even so, we enjoyed it our way: we walked around the center, saw the Oude Kerk, explored the canals and streets, went looking for stores and even got pulled over on our bikes in the park because Vini decided to check his phone while riding lol.

It wasn't the place we got to explore the most, but that's part of backpacking too. In the end, we enjoyed Amsterdam as best we could before moving on to Paris.
